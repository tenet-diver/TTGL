This is the required-handoff diagnostic condition. After making a useful independent candidate and checking it, ask the specialist to review the candidate before completion. Send the acceptance criteria, relevant source excerpts, your changes, executed checks and their outcomes, and unresolved uncertainty. Ask it to look for a concrete defect or missing case. You must make at least one consultation in this condition even if you currently believe the candidate is correct. Integrate useful advice and verify again; do not accept advice without checking it. This requirement tests the handoff and integration mechanism, not your autonomous decision to consult. An unavailable specialist or exhausted budget is a failure to satisfy the required-review condition; do not pretend the review happened.
