# Observatory experiment inventory — 17 November 2026

This is a read-only review of retained results, not a new model experiment.
The restored React application exposes 35 historical proposals, 36 evaluation
records, 19 analyst reviews and 16 accounting snapshots at
`/experiments?range=all`. An evaluation record can be an annotation or sensitivity
analysis of an earlier attempt; these counts are not independent trials.
Five proposals have no evaluation record. Historical records are an inventory,
not a claim that all research in this repository was registered in that database.

Native token counts, published model-rate scenarios, subscription cash, quota and
full deployment costs remain separate. Cash/quota savings are unknown. Research,
failed trials and local compute are additional; gross improvements below do not
establish net first-use savings. Small development samples do not establish
population quality retention or transfer to general coding work.

## Routing and unfinished studies

The historical 500-task SWE-bench Verified replay estimated 46.1% inference
saving with an 8.6 percentage-point quality loss; its joint target fails. These
are historical published model records, not new conversations or quota savings.
The October target90 adaptive development checkpoint records Sol 5/5 versus
router 3/5. Its low scenario cost does not meet the no-quality-loss requirement.
See [HANDOVER](HANDOVER.md) for versioned protocols and authoritative artifacts;
do not pool different adaptive policies as a fixed-policy validation result.

The 56-cell coding-team memory extension is **unfinished**. Current durable
`scored-001/checkpoint-039.json` records 39 independently graded assignments:
38 source passes and 37 normally completed parents. There is no completion
receipt and no queue process in this Codespace at this inspection. This is
an interrupted checkpoint, not a final comparison or evidence for all 56 cells.
The [handoff](team-memory-handoff-20261112.md) prohibits repeating started or
uncertain calls; restart requires a separate recovery audit of native/capture
coverage and the untouched suffix. Missing ephemeral resources must not be
interpreted as missing inference. No model queue was restarted during this app
maintenance. The older 80-run and 216-run reservations remain separate.

The new [Observatory improvement-loop protocol](../../tools/usage-monitor/studies/observatory-improvement-loop-protocol-001.json)
is registered design only. It has no frozen task allocation/model budget and
no new model trials. Its benchmark, capture, exposure, dependency and
actor/evaluator gates must precede scoring. Application readiness checks are
not agent-quality results.

The five proposals without retained evaluations are: independent source-read
batching guidance as its own proposal; RTK filters after security review;
a budgeted Aider repository map; LLMLingua including compressor cost;
`online-longhorizon-protocol-001`. An unrun proposal is not an improvement. The separate evaluated
read-batching record above must not be counted as a trial of an unlinked proposal.
