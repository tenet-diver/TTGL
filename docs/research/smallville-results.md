# Smallville memory: original, adaptations and measured results

8 November 2026. All registered mechanism, three-case development, direct original-style arm and six-case fresh memory-QA validation assignments are complete. The paced benchmark reference control also completed. This is not a long-horizon coding-agent efficacy result.

## Result

The original-style adapter retained the three development answers but failed the cost gate. The evidence-first candidate failed its separately frozen six-case validation gate. Keep original failures and the new result separate; no global memory or provider configuration was changed.

## What changed and what memory retained

The original native mechanism first exposed JSON-list and comma/full-subject tuple parsing failures. All failed snapshots and completed calls remain immutable. A deterministic V3 replay matched all 41 original prompts/roles and rebuilt valid triples with zero new LLM calls; exact recall remained correct. Synthetic useful records retained failed repair hypotheses, a newline diagnosis, the exact fix identifier and supporting regression checks. Fifteen evidence-linked reflections were produced only under a deliberately forced diagnostic trigger; the six events naturally left 135 importance points.

The separately labeled evidence-first candidate reuses self-hosted SQLite/FTS5 and reviewed local BGE. It keeps exact source chunks, IDs, offsets and hashes, combines lexical/dense retrieval, and permits the same raw-file fallback as controls. It removes remote per-item social scoring and proactive reflection. One public benchmark retrieval surfaced a previous campaign’s 2,000-person reach and a separate record about an influencer’s 10,000 followers, with source IDs retained for inspection. This combined change was evaluated; individual component effects were not isolated. It is retrieval/persistence, not weight training or a claim of better planning.

## Decision and limits

Retain the source-fidelity implementation and compatibility fixes for research. Reject the original Sol-scored adapter as the default on its measured economic result. Reject promotion of the evidence-first candidate under the frozen quality/cost gate; do not tune it using these validation outcomes. Six fresh stratified public questions, same-model judging and common background history do not establish population noninferiority or general coding-task gains. Readers used fresh native sessions; continued-thread cache/compaction economics were not evaluated. The disputed preference recommendation emphasized seasonal food/backstage context while the reference covered broader ride/event/show preferences. That is post-hoc qualitative diagnosis, not a causal explanation or a replacement grade; the permissive preference rubric makes rater sensitivity important. No held-out answer was rerun or used to modify the candidate. The original arm was a separate, later development run rather than a randomized simultaneous replicate.

All model counters are deduplicated native response measurements, including auxiliary compaction where present, reconciled against transport and Langfuse. Cached input is a subset of input. Credits use the frozen 7 November published-rate scenario, not invoices, subscription cash or quota. All original scoring/reflection/integration inference is included; judges, mechanism and research overhead remain separate. Local embedding runtime/calls are measured, their monetary/energy cost is unknown. No full-deployment or net first-use saving claim.

Observed study research lower bound: 4,815,960 native tokens, 115.36792 scenario credits across 265 native processes/298 unique responses, plus unaccounted parent/setup overhead. Recorded local embedding operations: 4,248. All registered study responses reconcile; no automatic process retries or schedules were introduced.
